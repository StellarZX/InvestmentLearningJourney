## What It Invests In

This fund tracks the Hang Seng Index through a QDII structure. It provides broad exposure to major Hong Kong listed companies, including financials, internet platforms, consumer names, and other large-cap Hong Kong market constituents.

## Portfolio Role

- Broad Hong Kong market allocation.
- Complements 021457 by adding more standard Hang Seng Index exposure.
- Useful as a core Hong Kong equity sleeve when held with a multi-year horizon.

## Key Risks

- QDII funds can be affected by quota, cross-market holidays, exchange rates, and tracking differences.
- The Hang Seng Index can remain undervalued for long periods.
- Large-cap Hong Kong exposure is sensitive to China macro expectations and global liquidity.
