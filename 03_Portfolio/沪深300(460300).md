## What It Invests In

This fund is an ETF feeder fund linked to the CSI 300 Index. The CSI 300 Index represents 300 large and liquid A-share companies listed in Shanghai and Shenzhen, and is commonly used as a core broad-market benchmark for China A-shares.

## Portfolio Role

- Core China equity allocation.
- Broad exposure to large-cap A-share companies.
- Suitable for disciplined long-term DCA instead of short-term market timing.
- Works as the China sleeve in a multi-market portfolio.

## Key Risks

- A-share market volatility can be high even for broad-market indices.
- CSI 300 exposure is biased toward large-cap sectors and may underperform smaller-cap or thematic indices in some cycles.
- Valuation, policy expectations, domestic liquidity, and earnings growth all affect returns.
