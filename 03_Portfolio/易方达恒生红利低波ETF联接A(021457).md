## What It Invests In

This fund is a feeder fund for a Hong Kong high-dividend low-volatility ETF strategy. Its target exposure is not the full Hang Seng Index. It focuses on Hong Kong Stock Connect eligible companies with relatively high dividend characteristics and lower historical volatility.

## Portfolio Role

- Defensive Hong Kong equity allocation.
- Complements broad Hang Seng exposure by reducing dependence on high-growth technology stocks.
- Intended for long-term DCA rather than short-term trading.

## Key Risks

- It is still an equity fund and can have material drawdowns.
- High dividend strategies may underperform during growth-led rallies.
- Sector concentration can be higher than a broad-market index.
- Hong Kong market sentiment, China macro expectations, interest rates, and currency movements can all affect returns.
