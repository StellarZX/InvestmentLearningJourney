## What It Invests In

SXR8 is a UCITS ETF that tracks the S&P 500 Index. It provides exposure to large-cap US companies across sectors such as technology, financials, healthcare, consumer discretionary, industrials, and communication services.

## Portfolio Role

- Core US equity allocation.
- Broad exposure to the largest listed US companies.
- Suitable for long-term global index investing.
- Accumulating share class reinvests dividends inside the fund instead of distributing cash.

## Key Risks

- US equity valuations can stay high and still experience sharp drawdowns.
- The S&P 500 has meaningful concentration in large technology and AI-related companies.
- Returns for a EUR-based investor are affected by EUR/USD exchange-rate movements.
- A single-market US allocation should be balanced with non-US exposure over time.
