# Lesson 15 — Compound Interest

> *"Small growth becomes powerful when it is given enough time."*

---

# Introduction

In the previous part, we learned how major stock markets are connected.

Now we move from markets to investment principles.

The first principle is compound interest.

Many beginners think investing depends mainly on finding the perfect stock or buying at the perfect time.

Long-term investing is built on a simpler idea:

money can grow on top of money that has already grown.

This is compounding.

---

## Why Compounding Matters

If you invest 10,000 RMB and earn 8% in one year, you have 10,800 RMB.

If the money remains invested and earns 8% again, the second year return is not based only on the original 10,000 RMB.

It is based on 10,800 RMB.

Your previous gain also starts working for you.

At first, the difference seems small.

Over many years, it can become very large.

This is why compounding is often described as the engine of long-term wealth.

---

## Time Is Your Greatest Asset

Compounding needs time.

This is why young investors have a special advantage.

They may not have much money at the beginning, but they can give their money many years to grow.

A person who starts early can sometimes end with more wealth than a person who starts later with larger monthly contributions.

The reason is not magic.

It is time.

The longer money stays invested, the more years it has to earn returns on previous returns.

---

## Compounding Is Not Smooth

Investment compounding is not like a bank deposit with a fixed interest rate.

Stock markets rise and fall.

An ETF may gain in one year, decline in the next year, and grow again later.

This can feel uncomfortable.

However, long-term compounding does not require every year to be positive.

It requires staying invested through normal market cycles.

If an investor sells every time the market falls, compounding is interrupted.

---

## The Cost of Waiting

Many beginners say, "I will start investing when I know more," or "I will start when the market is safer."

Learning is important.

But waiting for perfect certainty can delay the most valuable part of investing: time in the market.

This does not mean beginners should rush into risky products.

It means a simple, reasonable, long-term plan started early can be powerful.

Small amounts invested regularly can become meaningful because compounding works slowly at first and faster later.

---

## Summary

Compound interest means earning returns on previous returns.

It is one of the main reasons long-term investing can build wealth.

The most important ingredient is time.

Compounding is not smooth in the stock market, because prices rise and fall.

But with patience, diversification, and a long-term plan, investors can allow compounding to work.

In the next lesson, we will study inflation again in more detail.

Compounding helps money grow, but inflation quietly reduces what money can buy.
