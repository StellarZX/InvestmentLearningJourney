# Financial Glossary

## Day 1

### Market Index

A measurement that tracks the performance of a group of stocks.

Example: The CSI 300 is one of the most important market indices in China.


### ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)

An investment fund that tracks an index and can be traded on a stock exchange.

Example: Many long-term investors prefer ETFs over individual stocks.


### Track

To follow the performance of an index.

Example: This ETF tracks the S&P 500 Index.


### Market Sentiment

The overall mood of investors.

Example: Weak market sentiment led to a decline in the index.


### Advance

When the price of a stock increases.

Example: 120 stocks advanced today.


### Decline

When the price of a stock decreases.

Example: More stocks declined than advanced.


### Portfolio

A collection of investments owned by an investor.

Example: A diversified portfolio can reduce risk.

### Diversification

Spreading investments across different assets to reduce risk.

Example: Diversification is one of the basic principles of investing.


### Expense Ratio

The annual management fee charged by an ETF or mutual fund.


### Tracking Error

The difference between an ETF's performance and the index it follows.
